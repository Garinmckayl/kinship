# Kinship competition film design

## Concept

**The invisible care list becomes a closed loop.** Repetitive obligations arrive as
small paper-like task chips. Kinship gathers them into one calm teal current,
completes the machine work, and pauses at coral human-decision gates. The carrier
element is a continuous sea-glass path connecting every scene.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#EAFBF7` sea-glass mist
- Canvas cool: `#DDF6F3`
- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#082F2D`
- Ink soft: `#315B57`
- Teal: `#0F9F8F`
- Teal bright: `#42D7C1`
- Mint: `#BDF4E8`
- Coral: `#FF6B72` human urgency only
- Amber: `#F4A62A` waiting or attention
- Line: `#B9DED8`
- Technical dark: `#063A38`

Use controlled radial and mesh gradients between canvas colors. White cards must
retain visible teal-gray borders and soft shadows. Never place pale text over pale
browser footage.

## Typography

- Display: Archivo Black 400
- Human/editorial voice: EB Garamond 400/700
- UI and narration: Montserrat 400/700/900
- Technical labels: JetBrains Mono 400/700

## Composition

- Open on evidence, not a logo.
- Use one dominant visual claim per shot.
- Product captures sit in substantial browser/device frames, never tiny mosaics.
- Browser proof uses full-frame crops plus a dark-teal status rail with white text.
- Results become designed receipts with source, timestamp, fields, and action.
- Architecture uses three understandable planes: human context, Strands reasoning,
  and durable AWS action infrastructure.
- Captions overlay the true frame at the lower safe area and never displace content.

## Motion

- Film current: leftward task flow becomes inward depth during live work.
- Repetitive task chips waterfall into a single queue.
- Human decisions stop the current for a deliberate beat before approval.
- Live Nova proof uses a sustained camera push and cursor-led focus.
- Receipts arrive through inverse zoom-through from browser evidence.
- Stillness is reserved for the verified provider receipt and final promise.
- Motion must perform work; no breathing, floating, or idle wobble.
