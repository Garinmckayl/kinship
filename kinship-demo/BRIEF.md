## Intent

A competition film that makes the hidden labor of family caregiving tangible, then
shows Kinship quietly completing repetitive care work end to end. The film opens
with the strongest real outcome: Nova Act working on Medicare.gov and returning
verified nearby clinicians. It then reveals why that work matters to Eleanor and
Sarah, demonstrates medication, health-context, approval, browser, and appointment
loops, and closes with the AWS architecture and credible human impact.

## Competition thesis

Kinship is an Everyday Agent built around the competition's ideal behavior:
it runs quietly in the background and only surfaces when there is a real decision
to make. It checks, remembers, searches, follows up, and verifies. Humans retain
control over consequential actions.

## Assets

- `assets/captures/nova-live-proof.mp4` — authentic production recording of Nova Act
  working through Medicare.gov in an AgentCore Browser.
- `assets/captures/provider-completed.png` — source evidence for the verified result.
- Authentic production captures of Eleanor's medication view, health trends,
  calendar, family dashboard, and caregiver approval flow.
- `assets/voice/voiceover-v3.mp3` narration, starting at 1s, with `assets/voice/voiceover-v3.words.json` used for caption timing.

## Visual direction

- Bright, premium light-teal identity: airy aqua canvas, white glass, deep teal ink,
  sea-glass gradients, coral reserved for human urgency.
- The film should feel humane and trustworthy, not like a dark cybersecurity demo.
- Real browser proof fills the frame and remains readable. Status text uses strong
  contrast and a designed progress rail.
- Raw JSON never serves as the result. Structured source data becomes a clear,
  source-labeled receipt while the authentic capture remains evidence.
- Captions remain a quiet overlay rail; no caption-safe bottom band.

## Story requirements

1. Create an emotional response in the first ten seconds.
2. Name repetitive invisible care work explicitly.
3. Demonstrate who Kinship serves and why the problem matters.
4. Show several truthful care loops, with one clearly marked live Nova proof.
5. Show human approval before consequential external action.
6. Build a technically rich Strands, Bedrock, AgentCore, Nova Act, and application
   architecture that remains understandable.
7. End with measurable potential impact and a memorable vision.

## Truth boundary

- Do not imply diagnosis, autonomous medical decisions, or fabricated results.
- Do not present capability templates as completed production transactions.
- Label the Medicare provider search as the live Nova proof.
- Label refill, bill, benefit, and appointment browser playbooks as approval-gated
  workflows unless each is independently captured running.
- Keep the finished film below Devpost's five-minute limit.
